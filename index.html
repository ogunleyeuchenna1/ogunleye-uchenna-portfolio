02 / WELLNESS

ONNE

A multi-location massage and wellness booking platform for managing appointments, therapists, and rooms.

Technical details, challenges & solutions

Project: ZenSpace · Role: Full-Stack Developer
Stack: React / Next.js, Node.js / Express, PostgreSQL, Redis, Tailwind CSS, Stripe, Twilio, SendGrid.

Engineering highlights

  • Scheduling resolves therapist availability, room capacity by location, and service duration, with configurable 15-minute cleaning buffers.
  • Supports pricing by room tier, therapist seniority, and location; Stripe webhooks process payments and deposits while holding booking slots.
  • BullMQ on Redis queues confirmations and 24-hour reminders through Twilio SMS and SendGrid email.

Challenges & solutions

Double-booking: Atomic database transactions and Redis-backed temporary session locks protect simultaneous reservations.

Slow availability queries: PostgreSQL indexes and cached therapist availability bring schedule query latency below 40ms.

03 / FINTECH

VERTEXGOLD

A trading platform for monitoring market activity and strategies with real-time performance charts.

Technical details, challenges & solutions

Project: AlgoTrader · Role: Full-Stack Developer & Systems Architect
Stack: React / Next.js, Node.js or Python FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, WebSockets, TradingView API, Stripe.

Engineering highlights

  • Live analytics and interactive backtesting charts track win/loss ratio, Sharpe ratio, and drawdown with TradingView Lightweight Charts and WebSockets.
  • Exchange API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256, with trade-only permission validation.
  • Sub-second TradingView webhook signals are queued through Redis pub/sub for asynchronous exchange-order processing; Stripe Billing manages subscriptions and plan access.

Challenges & solutions

Stream latency and dropped connections during volatile market events: Redis caching and connection pooling aggregate ticker streams for hundreds of dashboards, reducing client payload bandwidth by 65% and keeping chart rendering under 50ms.

Duplicate orders on retries: Short-lived Redis signal-hash keys make webhook handling idempotent.

04 / PRODUCTIVITY

LINKPAD

A browser-based companion for moving files, sharing clipboard text, and typing across devices.

Technical details, challenges & solutions

Role: Full-Stack Developer & Systems Architect
Stack: Next.js / React, Node.js, Socket.io / WebSockets / WebRTC, Redis, Tailwind CSS.

Engineering highlights

  • Transfers photos, documents, and archives with live progress, and syncs clipboard text between devices.
  • Turns a phone into a remote laptop keyboard, with QR-code or short-code pairing and no desktop install.

Challenges & solutions

Lag over unstable connections: Event debouncing and lightweight WebSocket payloads bring keystroke latency below 20ms on local networks.

Large-file memory use: ReadableStream processing and chunked ArrayBuffers stream files over sockets without loading the entire transfer into browser memory.
